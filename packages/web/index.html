Selector wand

Click a block to select it, shift-click another to select the box between them, click the ground to target an empty cell. Drag to orbit. Undo with Ctrl/Cmd+Z.
Walk mode: WASD to move, mouse to look. Walking: Space jumps, Shift sneaks. Double-tap Space to fly or drop; flying, Space/Shift rise and sink, and touching the ground lands you. Left click selects at the crosshair, right click grows the box. With the wheel open, flick the mouse toward an action and click, or press its key. Esc or E frees the cursor: then drag to look and click to select, as in orbit view. E captures the mouse again.
