      Blocks

        Hotbar below: keys 1-9. Build tool B: right-click places, left-click breaks, middle-click picks.

        Tools

        Click a block to select it, shift-click another to select the box between them, click the ground to target an empty cell. Drag to orbit. Undo with Ctrl/Cmd+Z.
        Walk mode: WASD to move, mouse to look. Walking: Space jumps, Shift sprints at double speed. Double-tap Space to fly or drop; flying, Space/Shift rise and sink and Ctrl sprints at double speed, and touching the ground lands you. Left click selects at the crosshair, right click grows the box. With the wheel open, flick the mouse toward an action and click, or press its key. Esc or E frees the cursor: then drag to look and click to select, as in orbit view. E captures the mouse again.

        Build prep

            Building Gadgets: open a Template Manager with a Copy-Paste Gadget (or paper) in it and press Paste.
            Turtle: put the schema on the turtle and run factory <file>. Tick items off as you gather them; ticks are saved in this browser.
            A turtle built by cc-factory is mirrored left to right and sits behind-left of its start. Blueprint coordinates are shown as designed.
