# Casino main hall: design brief

Status: mapping. Nothing is built yet. Decisions below were agreed in conversation; open items are at the end.

## Concept

A grand art deco casino and entertainment complex. Guests arrive at a veranda and covered porch, pass through a vestibule into a lobby, and walk a curving concourse (a horseshoe around an open atrium) with games along the way. A skybridge crosses the atrium on the second floor.

- Style: art deco, gold and black (gold blocks, polished blackstone, quartz, glowstone).
- Overall scale: about 80 wide by 100 deep including the entrance, 24 high.
- Modular: each game is designed separately and dropped into a standard bay.

- Concourse: about 14 wide, half-ring, centerline radius about 36 (about 110 blocks long).
- Atrium: open void on the inner side, balustrade on floor 2.
- Skybridge: a straight chord across the atrium at floor 2 height, with a view of the derby screen.
- Gallery (floor 2): ring above the concourse. VIP rooms, a bar, overflow rooms.

## Entrance sequence (south)

1. Veranda: open terrace, about 50 wide and 10 deep, wide steps, balustrades, lamp posts.
2. Covered porch: roofed portico, about 20 wide and 8 deep, tall columns with gold bands, porte-cochere.
3. Vestibule: short double-height lobby with large doors, coat and cashier areas.
4. Lobby: opens onto the concourse under the skybridge.

## Bay socket contract

Every bay provides the same things, so a game blueprint can be stamped in without changing the hall:

- Door: a fixed arch position and width onto the concourse, at a fixed floor level.
- Interior: fixed footprint and ceiling height, walls and floor already in the hall style.
- Wiring channel: a fixed route in for power and network cables.
- Origin: one marked corner, so game blueprint coordinates line up.

Bay sizes: S 12x12x8, M 16x16x10, L 16x24x12. The curve of the concourse is made of voxel steps; bays stay rectangular and small wedge alcoves fill the gaps.

## Cabinet module (3x3x3)

Front face, standing in front:

- Top two rows: monitor (2 high x 3 wide).
- Bottom row: button deck.
- Behind the monitor: the computer. Wiring runs behind it.
- Buttons connect to the computer and emit redstone.
- The signal stays inside the cabinet.

Needs to be built once as its own blueprint and reused (slots, betting terminals).

## Screens

- A single CC:Tweaked monitor is, by default, at most 8 wide by 6 high. Check the server config.
- Larger walls are tiled panels (for example 3x2 of 8x6 gives 24x12). One computer can drive several; seams will show.
- Theater video is a software question for later (pre-converted frames or a stream). The hall provides the screen wall and sightlines.

## Turtle testing

- A test must fit in about 1000 blocks. Test in modules: one bay plus a concourse segment, the cabinet, the veranda and porch.
- Redstone dust will not place by turtle, and doors and fluids will not place. The cabinet's wiring needs an approach that avoids dust.

## Massing (generated by `npx tsx tools/gen-casino.ts`)

Shapes are boxes, not detailed builds. Blueprints: `casino-hall` (everything), `entrance`, and one `room-<slug>` per room at its own origin.

- The hall is a rectilinear horseshoe, not a true arc: concourse legs on the west, north and east, closed by the lobby on the south. Bays stay axis-aligned.
- Site is about 108 x 109 (x 6..113, z 6..108), 24 high, plus the entrance (about 26 deep). The 80-wide figure above was too small once rooms sit around the ring.
- Ground floor: derby bar, grand stair and theater (north leg, derby and theater double height); cashier, slots and blackjack (west leg); shut-the-box, golf sim and dungeon stub (east leg).
- Floor 2: VIP rooms (west), baseball, faceball and bowling (east), over the ground bays. Gallery ring, skybridge across the atrium at z 58..62.
- The derby bar sits at the west end of the north leg with its screen facing east down the north concourse.
- Room shells list outer sizes; interior is 2 less on each axis.

## Decisions

- Popcorn: All the Mods 10 provides `pamhc2foodextended:popcornitem` (Pam's HarvestCraft 2 Food Extended). It is an item, so the theater counter hands it out from a hopper or dispenser; detail it with the theater lobby.
- Derby screen: as big as possible, facing down the concourse.
- Cabinet buttons connect to the computer's front, left and right.
